ROBOT 2026 · Iberian Robotics Conference

DTF-Track: Dual-Tier Fusion for People Tracking

A unified ROS 2 node for robust 2D-LiDAR people tracking. Three laser detectors must agree before a new track is created, and YOLO only confirms the class, so the camera never moves a track.

−47%excess IDs in clutter
−72%excess IDs with occlusions
2×precision vs. SPENCER

About

I build perception and decision-making systems that let robots move and interact safely and naturally around people.

I am a PhD researcher at the Institute of Robotics and Industrial Informatics (IRI, CSIC–UPC) in Barcelona, in the Mobile Robotics group. My work connects robust people detection and tracking, situational awareness and interaction-aware decisions, and I test it on real robots in shared human environments. The code is open-source on ROS 2.

Research focus

What I work on

From raw sensor data to socially-aware behaviour, on real robots.

People detection & tracking

Robust 2D-LiDAR and RGB multi-person tracking under clutter, ego-motion and occlusions.

Socially-aware navigation

Robots that move through dynamic human environments while respecting people's space and intentions.

Perception–awareness–decision

Architectures that turn multimodal perception into situational awareness and interaction-aware decisions.

Grounded HRI & language

Context-grounded dialogue and vision–language models for task-level human–robot interaction.

Cross-platform evaluation

Reproducible benchmarking of perception across robots and in simulation (ROS 2, Gazebo).

Semantic scene understanding

Semantic mapping and multimodal scene understanding for robots in shared spaces.

DTF-Track: Dual-Tier Fusion for People Tracking

E. Bejarano, V. Bo, A. Garrell, A. Sanfeliu

Consensus-based multi-detector fusion and LiDAR-first Kalman tracking in a single ROS 2 node. It produces 43–72% fewer excess identities than SPENCER.
